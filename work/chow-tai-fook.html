02 / Selected Work

Rethinking O2O
across 7,000+ stores.

Client

Chow Tai Fook

Role

Strategy & Transformation

Focus

O2O · Customer Experience · Digital Ecosystem

The Challenge

How do you rethink the customer experience when digital and physical retail need to work as one system?

Context

Chow Tai Fook operates one of the largest jewellery retail networks in Greater China, with more than 7,000 stores.

The challenge wasn't simply to improve a website or add another digital touchpoint. It was to understand how customers, retail teams and digital platforms interacted across the broader journey — and where those experiences were breaking down.

My Role

I led the strategy work with a small consulting team, working across customer research, stakeholder interviews, journey mapping, ecosystem analysis and executive workshops.

What We Did

01

Talked to customers to understand how the experience worked across retail teams and internal stakeholders.

02

Mapped the ecosystem to identify gaps across online and offline touchpoints.

03

Defined the opportunities across customer experience and supporting platforms.

04

Aligned leadership through three workshops on priorities, trade-offs and future direction.

05

Set the transformation direction across five integrated digital platforms.

The Outcome

The work created a shared view of the future O2O experience and a transformation direction connecting five digital platforms across a retail network of 7,000+ stores.
